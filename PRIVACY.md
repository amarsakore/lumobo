# Lumobo privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Lumobo is a photo editor that works entirely on your device.

**What Lumobo collects:** nothing. Lumobo has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting, and it does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

**Your photos.** Lumobo asks for permission to read the photos on your device so it can show your library and open photos for editing. Photos are only read and processed on your phone. Edited photos are saved to your device's gallery (in the *Lumobo* album). If you choose to keep camera details or location in Settings, that information is copied from the original photo into the edited one and stays on your device.

**Settings and saved looks** are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

**Sharing.** If you share a photo from Lumobo, it is handed to the app you choose through Android's share sheet; what happens next is governed by that app.

**Children.** Lumobo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published here.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/amarsakore/lumobo/issues
